# Sardal: Sovereign Arabic-Islamic AI Operating System

Welcome to the official documentation and company overview for **Sardal** and **Cohesion Holding**.

## 🌍 About Sardal

**Sardal** is a fully sovereign, Arabic-Islamic AI operating system designed to:

- ✅ Protect cognitive sovereignty for Arab and Islamic nations
- ✅ Maintain cultural identity and Islamic values in AI systems
- ✅ Provide independent digital infrastructure free from foreign control
- ✅ Enable Arabic natural language processing with unprecedented depth
- ✅ Embed Maqasid (Islamic objectives) directly into AI decision-making

## 🏢 Cohesion Holding Structure

Our multi-tier organizational structure ensures:

- **IP Protection:** All intellectual property owned and protected at the parent level
- **Operational Flexibility:** Multiple subsidiaries for government, commercial, and research operations
- **Scalable Growth:** Each subsidiary operates independently while sharing core technology
- **Investor-Friendly:** Clear governance enabling external investment without diluting founder control
