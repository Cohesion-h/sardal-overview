# Business Model & Licensing Strategy

**Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** 2026-05-14

---

## Executive Summary

Cohesion Holding operates a **multi-tier licensing revenue model** where:

1. **Holding Company** (Cohesion Holding) owns IP and issues licenses
2. **Operating Subsidiaries** (Sardal Operations, Labs, Capital) execute services/research
3. **Revenue Flows:** Operating subsidiaries → Licensing fees to parent → IP licensing to external partners

This structure enables sustainable revenue diversification while maintaining founder control and enabling phased investment.

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### 5.1 Pricing Models

#### **Model 1: Subscription (Per User / Per Deployment)**
- Monthly/Annual fee based on usage levels
- Example: $10–100/month depending on transaction volume
- Suitable for: SaaS-delivered services, API usage

#### **Model 2: Percentage of Revenue (Licensee's Revenue)**
- License fee = X% of licensee's annual revenue from product using Sardal
- Example: 5–15% of partner company's revenue
- Advantages: Aligns incentives, scales with partner success
- Suitable for: Commercial partners, integrations

#### **Model 3: Fixed Annual License (Enterprise)**
- Flat annual fee (e.g., 50–200 KWD)
- Advantages: Predictable cost for licensee, simplicity
- Suitable for: Government contracts, large enterprises

#### **Model 4: Usage-Based (API Calls, Requests)**
- Price per API call or per 1000 requests
- Example: 0.001 KWD per API call
- Advantages: Customers pay only for what they use
- Suitable for: High-volume commercial integrations

### 5.2 Pricing by Customer Segment

| Segment | Model | Annual Price (Example) | Justification |
|---------|-------|----------------------|---------------|
| **Government** | Fixed annual or revenue % | 100–250 KWD | Long-term relationship, strategic value |
| **Enterprise** | Fixed annual | 20–100 KWD | Predictability, commitment |
| **Mid-Market** | Usage-based + floor | 10–50 KWD + overage | Flexibility, growth accommodation |
| **Startup/SMB** | Usage-based | 0.5–5 KWD | Low barrier to entry |
| **Academic** | Free–minimal | 1–5 KWD | Research value, talent pipeline |
